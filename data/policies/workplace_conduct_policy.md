# Workplace Conduct and Reporting Policy

## Reporting Concerns
The company prohibits harassment, discrimination, retaliation, violence, and threats. Employees may report a concern to People Operations, a manager, or the confidential reporting channel. Reports are handled as privately as practical and are reviewed promptly.

## Non-Retaliation and Emergencies
Retaliation for making a good-faith report or participating in a review is prohibited. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. The agent must not investigate, make findings, or promise confidentiality; it should route the issue to trained HR staff.
