Answer rubric accuracy requires the case's explicit required claims and the configured gold-reference term coverage. Citation coverage now requires **every** declared policy document, not just one document from a multi-policy question. Tool coverage similarly requires every declared tool but does not penalise legitimate additional safety tools.

Groundedness is an automatic proxy: it checks required-document coverage, citation shape, and (in local mode) that citation IDs resolve to the index. It cannot establish that every sentence is faithfully entailed by a cited chunk; retain human review for that question.

Synthetic per-case answers, citations, tool traces, expected claims, and scores are saved in `evaluation/artifacts.json` for human review.

In HTTP mode, citation IDs are not claimed to resolve unless the deployment exposes its index; document coverage and citation structure are still measured from the API response.
